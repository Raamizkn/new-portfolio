# Product Context: New Portfolio

## Why this project exists
The portfolio serves as a central hub for Raamiz Khan Niazi's professional identity, demonstrating expertise in AI, Product Management, and Development.

## Problems it solves
- Fragmented professional information across different platforms.
- Need for a platform to host deep-dive case studies that go beyond a simple resume.
- Establishing credibility in the AI and Product Management space.

## How it should work
- Users can navigate through different sections of the portfolio.
- Users can filter projects by category to see relevant work.
- Users can click on "Product Management" projects to view in-depth case studies with strategic analysis, market data, and product frameworks.
- Users can easily find contact information and links to external profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn).

## User Experience Goals
- Clean, minimalist aesthetic with focus on typography and high-quality visuals.
- Smooth transitions and interactive elements using Framer Motion.
- Intuitive navigation across both desktop and mobile devices.
- High readability for long-form case study content.
