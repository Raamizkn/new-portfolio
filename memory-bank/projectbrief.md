# Project Brief: New Portfolio

Core requirements and goals for the portfolio website.

## Goals
- Showcase professional experience in AI Product Management and Software Development.
- Present detailed case studies for product management projects.
- Provide a modern, responsive, and visually appealing user interface.
- Enable easy contact for potential clients and recruiters.

## Scope
- Personal branding and hero section.
- About section highlighting key focus areas.
- Projects section with category filtering (Product Management, Software Development).
- Detailed case study pages for major projects.
- Skills and expertise visualization.
- Professional journey (experience) and certifications.
- Contact form and social links.
